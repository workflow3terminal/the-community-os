Privacy Policy

The Community OS processes membership and game data to run Focus Points, quests, proof, ranks, and governance. Staging may keep data on your device (local-first) and/or on free-tier hosts later.

Data

Account identity (when auth exists), display name, pathway, FP/wallet, posts, reactions, quest proofs, Court/audit rows, and technical logs needed to operate the service. Optional analytics only with separate opt-in. FP is not money. We do not sell your data as ads.

Sharing

Court roles may see proofs they must decide. Hosting/auth processors see what is required to serve the app. No Tribunal-restricted material on public pages.

Your rights

Export and deletion are product requirements (soft-delete then hard-delete PII; anonymized ledger aggregates may remain). GDPR-style rights apply where the law does. Clear site data / Reset demo removes local staging state.

Transfers & age

Free hosts (e.g. GitHub Pages) may process data outside the EU under vendor terms. Service is for users who meet digital consent age. Operator contact to be published before production cutover.
